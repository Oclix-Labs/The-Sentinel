school 00 — What is RWA?

Redefining Asset Transparency

Real-World Assets (RWAs) are the next frontier of blockchain utility — US Treasuries, real estate, and private credit brought on-chain for 24/7 liquidity and global access. The market is at ~$29.7B as of April 2026 (Source: RWA.xyz, 16 Apr 2026, Distributed Asset Value), with tokenized US Treasuries alone at ~$13.5B (~50× growth since early 2024).

But every RWA and DeFi price on Base depends on oracles — and oracles fail. RWA Sentinel is the neutral, independent watchdog that cross-checks them so retail users don't have to trust a single feed.

help_outline 02 — Problem & Solution
warning

A Pattern, Not an Incident

Over the past 18 months, at least nine oracle-composition or hardcoded-oracle failures across Base-adjacent DeFi have caused ≥$50M in losses — roughly one incident every two months. The most recent is the Moonwell cbETH incident of February 2026 ($2.68M impacting ~181 borrowers) caused by an OEV wrapper misconfiguration. Every single one of those incidents would have been caught by a multi-oracle ±2% cross-check in the same block it occurred. This is a systemic failure mode, not a one-off.

bolt

The Sentinel Solution

An always-on cross-check of Chainlink, Pyth, and RedStone for 5 core Base assets on a 1-minute cadence. When deviation exceeds ±2%, the event is queued, fanned out to Telegram/webhook subscribers, and written to AlertRegistry on Base Mainnet as an append-only record. Every line of code is MIT-licensed; every alert is publicly verifiable on-chain.

architecture 03 — Architecture

Edge Workers + On-Chain Registry

Cross-check runs on Cloudflare Workers (~$55/mo at scale vs ~$8.7K/mo fully on-chain). Alerts are delivered off-chain for speed and anchored on-chain for auditability.

Known Limitations & Mitigations
  • - cbETH is dual-sourced, not triple: only Chainlink (OEV-wrapped post-Feb 2026) and Pyth sponsored publish cbETH on Base. RedStone feed is not currently live for cbETH. Dual-source still catches the Moonwell-style misconfiguration in the same block.
  • - USDO uses Chainlink PoR, not price deviation: attestation tracking monitors AnswerUpdated staleness and reserve-vs-supply delta, not cross-oracle price agreement. Pyth/RedStone do not publish USDO attestations.
  • - Alert latency vs feed heartbeats: Our <60s target is the alert dispatch latency once a qualifying event is observed. Chainlink heartbeats (24h for PoR, event-driven for prices) determine how quickly the underlying feed updates.
  • - Centralized Phase 1: A single Oclix Labs-operated poller runs Phase 1. Phase 2 introduces 2–3 independent federated operators; Phase 3 opens permissionless staking.
